STEREOLOVE

Stereolove is a perceptual instrument. The screen is not treated as a window, but as a volume that opens when your viewpoint changes.

Move slowly. Hidden questions gather from scattered light when you find the right angle. The work asks how attention, memory, and viewpoint edit reality.

  1. Move your head to search for the hidden angle.
  2. Raise an open hand near your face to open another question.
  3. No camera? Use touch or mouse to explore the same space.
